# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or personal attacks.
- No publishing of others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or other contributions that break these rules,
and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub
([@neboki](https://github.com/neboki)). All reports are handled confidentially.
