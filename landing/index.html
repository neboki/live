Where it works

On your computer

Zoom, Teams, Meet, YouTube, webinars. The captions float in a window on top of everything, or stay in the app.

In the room

Meetings, interviews, classes. Put your phone or laptop on the table and read along: it listens through the microphone.

Both at once

The call and the room together: the shared tab and the microphone, mixed.

What you get

  • Follow along. The translation, with the original underneath if you want it, or read aloud.
  • Join in. What you could reply and what you could ask, in your language and theirs.
  • Skip the notes. A live summary, and the whole conversation to export.
  • Get the names right. A glossary of names and terms that must not be translated.
  • Keep the bill in check. A live cost estimate and a spending limit per session.

Why you can trust it

No server in between

Your browser sends the audio straight to OpenAI. Nothing passes through us.

Your key stays with you

It is kept in your browser, and only if you choose to remember it.

Open source

All the code is public, so anyone can check what it does. No analytics.

Start in three steps

  1. Get an OpenAI API key (or use your company's Azure OpenAI).
  2. Open the app, pick your language and what to listen to.
  3. Press Start, and read along.
