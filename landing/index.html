How it works

From sound to caption, in about a second

  1. Listens The tab, the mic, or both
  2. Cuts sentences At each natural pause
  3. Transcribes And detects the language
  4. Translates With your glossary
  5. Shows it On screen or in your ear

Private by design

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do the other people need to install anything?

No. Only you open Neboki Live. It listens to the call or the room, and nobody else needs to do anything.

Which languages does it understand?

It detects the spoken language by itself, sentence by sentence, so people can mix languages. It translates into more than 20, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Chinese, Japanese and Arabic.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in the browser. On a phone it listens through the microphone; add it to your home screen and it opens full screen, like an app.

Is my conversation stored anywhere?

Only in your browser, unless you sign in and choose to save it to your account. The audio goes to OpenAI to be transcribed and translated, under your own API terms.

Why do I need my own OpenAI key?

It is what keeps the app free and private: you pay the model directly, at cost, and no server of ours sits in the middle of your conversations.
